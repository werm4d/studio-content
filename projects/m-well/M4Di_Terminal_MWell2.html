Project Vision (Client Statement at Intake)
M-Well Medical Group seeks to develop a full-spectrum community health campus on the 10-acre former school site. Phase 1 converts the existing school building into licensed residential care for children and young adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD). Phase 2 adds a new 4-story Wellness Pavilion providing community medical, behavioral health, occupational therapy, and primary care services. The full campus vision integrates both buildings with a park-like healing landscape — outdoor therapeutic gardens, accessible walking trails, and public green space — serving as both a care environment and a community asset.
