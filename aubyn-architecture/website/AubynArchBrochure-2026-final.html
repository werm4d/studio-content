About the Firm
Architect. Landscape Designer.
Owner's Advocate.

Aubyn Architecture LLC is a multi-discipline professional architecture, landscape design, and consulting firm led by David C. Ainsworth AIA — a licensed architect with over 20 years of experience spanning residential, commercial, institutional, and healthcare design.

Registered in Delaware, Pennsylvania, and Connecticut, the firm provides full-service design from early feasibility through construction administration. Practice scale is deliberate: boutique, principal-led, every project.

We bring owner's-side project leadership that most design firms cannot offer — having managed programs at Yale New Haven Health and Temple University, we understand what institutional clients need when complexity increases.

Mission & Philosophy
Form,
Function,
Fusion.

Our work finds balance and harmony between the natural and built environment. Elegance in simplicity. Beauty in the subtle.

The universal elements — orientation, light, color, air, soil, water, fire, sight, sound, touch, and smell — are critical to our creative process.

A dedication to efficient resource use and zero-waste design is not a constraint. It is the discipline that consistently produces more thoughtful, enduring results.

"Design begins with listening and ends with buildings that endure."
Core Values
Public Health & Safety
Architects are obligated to protect the public interest — an obligation that now encompasses sustainability of the global environment and universal accessibility. We take that mandate seriously on every project we sign.
Design Excellence
Functional layouts. Energy-efficient construction. Environments that are visually compelling and increase market value while delivering genuine human benefit to occupants and community.
Client Advocacy
Architecture is self-regulated. Every decision is made in service of your interests, in accordance with the highest professional standards. We have sat on the client side — we know what that actually means.
