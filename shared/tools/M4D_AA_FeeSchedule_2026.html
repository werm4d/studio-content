Frequently Asked Questions & Service Descriptions
What does the architecture fee cover?▾
The architecture fee covers all AIA B101-2017 basic services from your first design meeting through the end of construction: schematic design, design development, construction documents (full permit-ready drawing set), bidding support, and construction administration (site visits, submittals, RFIs, punch list). Not included: MEP, structural, and civil engineering subconsultants (billed separately at cost + 10%), permit fees, surveys, geotechnical investigations, existing conditions documentation, code & life safety analysis, and predevelopment feasibility (M4Di OI Tool).
Does the fee include MEP and engineering?▾
No — and this is the most common misunderstanding in the industry. Every benchmark (DE OMB OMB22131, Siana, TXR, AIA) cites architecture prime fees only. MEP, structural, and civil are separate firms with separate fees, coordinated by M4D but billed independently. The full design team typically runs 10.5–18.5% of construction cost total. Our architecture fee is the largest piece; engineering adds 3–7% on top depending on project type.
What is a Pre-Inspection Observation (CPO)?▾
Trevor Knight (ICC Certified Building Official) or Leo Lynch (former Acting Director, Wilmington L&I) visits your jobsite before each AHJ inspection phase — footings, foundation, framing, rough-ins, pre-drywall, final. They produce a written corrective action list so your contractor fixes issues before the official inspector arrives. This is not available anywhere else. Trevor's CBO is the highest ICC certification — he trained the inspectors reviewing your building. A single failed inspection costs $2,000–$10,000+ in delays. The CPO typically pays for itself on the first visit.
  • Single residential visit + report: $650–$1,500
  • Full residential program (5–6 phases): $2,500–$4,000
  • Commercial single visit: $1,000–$2,500
  • Commercial program: $200–$250/hr NTE
What does Code & Life Safety Analysis include?▾
A standalone written code analysis memo suitable for permit pre-application or board presentation. Covers: occupancy classification, egress analysis (exits, travel distance, widths), fire protection overview (sprinkler requirements, fire separation), accessibility (ADA/ANSI A117.1), and energy code summary (IECC applicability). Fixed fees apply to small-to-medium facilities with clearly defined scope (≤30,000 SF). Larger facilities, mixed-occupancy complexes, licensed care, historic, or correctional projects: hourly NTE — too many variables for a defensible fixed fee. Trevor Knight (ICC CBO) leads all analysis.
What does existing conditions documentation include?▾
We visit the building, take physical measurements of every space, photograph all existing conditions, and produce a 2D CAD drawing set: floor plans (all levels), exterior elevations (all four sides), and building sections (minimum two). Architectural documentation only — MEP (ductwork, piping, electrical) is a separate field survey billed hourly. Fee is a sliding scale: $1.50/SF for buildings under 1,500 SF, stepping down to $0.85/SF for larger buildings. Minimum: $1,200. Industry comparison: professional architectural as-built documentation runs $0.75–$2.00/SF nationally (LiDAR As-Built Cost Guide 2026) — our rates reflect principal-architect labor, not offshore drafting.
What is the M4Di Predevelopment OI Tool?▾
M4Di's Operational Intelligence (OI) Tool is a proprietary predevelopment cost estimating system grounded in local bid data, BLS wage data for the DE/Philadelphia/NJ/Baltimore corridor, and ENR Construction Cost Index escalation. It produces a CSI-based cost estimate, $/SF benchmark comparison, and a clarifications & exclusions log. Three service tiers:
  • P1 — Zoning + ROM Budget ($8,000): Zoning + rough order of magnitude estimate. Board/CDFI-ready same day.
  • P2 — Test Fit + ROM Update ($14,000): Adds conceptual massing, test fit, updated ROM, multi-scenario cost comparison.
  • P3 — Detailed Estimate + RFP/RFQ ($22,000): Full CSI estimate, procurement documents, risk register, funder-ready package.
All tiers deliver outputs locked at ALDi Gate G0.
Can the fee be negotiated?▾
Here's where we can move and where we can't. Our architecture fees are benchmarked against the State of Delaware's published A&E fee schedule — that's the floor, and going below it means cutting scope. What we can do: structure phased billing for smaller upfront commitment; explore fixed-fee arrangements for well-defined scopes; discuss strategic partner rates for mission-aligned nonprofit clients. What we can't do: discount engineering pass-throughs — those are at cost + 10% with no margin to cut. The fee difference between M4D and a cheaper firm typically pays for itself in the first AHJ comment cycle we prevent.
