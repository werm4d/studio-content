The Difference

Community projects deserve a real budget before they need a design.

Too many nonprofits, CDCs and small developers reach the design team with funding secured and a deadline attached — but a budget never tied to an actual scope. The project starts underfunded, fees and services get cut, and the building is value-engineered away from its purpose.

Merge 4 Design changes that sequence. We define the project, its true cost and its approval pathway first — so funders can underwrite a real number and owners can hire any architect with a clear scope.

What Owners Get

A go / no-go answer early

A benchmark budget and a clear decision before design fees are spent.

Fewer approval surprises

Zoning, code and permitting issues flagged early — not at the counter.

Right-sized, senior-led team

Accountable, and built to fit the project — not padded for overhead.

Wilmington code fluency

Deep roots in the local building and policy community mean we understand how review actually works — on paper and in practice.

Signature Service

M4Di FeasOps™ — fixed-price feasibility.

A benchmark budget funders and lenders can underwrite, produced before an architect is hired. Using publicly available site and property data, each tier ends in a go / no-go document you can attach to a funding request — or hand to any architect with a clearly defined scope.

Powered by M4Di cost intelligence: CSI-structured ROM estimates, capital-stack modeling (NMTC, LIHTC, CDFI and grant sources) and Mid-Atlantic community-development cost benchmarks.

Pre-Inspection Construction Reviews

An ICC Certified Building Official reviews the work before each City inspection and issues a written corrective-action report.

  • Footings, foundation, framing, rough-ins
  • Pre-drywall and pre-close visits
  • Per review, or one lump sum for every scheduled inspection

Advisory; does not replace City of Wilmington inspections.

Our Process

Discover. Decide. Deliver.

01

Discover

Align goals, constraints and stakeholders fast.

  • Site and context review
  • Existing conditions and as-built capture
  • Early zoning, code, ADA and life-safety flags
  • Decision priorities: budget, schedule, approvals, operations
02

Decide

Put a real number and a clear pathway in front of you.

  • Go / no-go recommendation with cost and funding
  • Permitting and entitlement strategy
  • Phasing options for occupied or funded-in-stages projects
  • ROM cost and schedule: range-based, reality-based
03

Deliver

Support your design team and keep construction on track.

  • Specification and document-production support
  • Stakeholder and agency coordination
  • Pre-inspection reviews before each City inspection
  • Fewer surprises, fewer rework loops
Senior leadership stays involved — from first conversation through final inspection.
